## 🏥 Project Summary

**AI Doctor** is a next-generation AI-powered healthcare assistant platform designed to help users access instant medical insights, analyze health documents, understand symptoms, and discover nearby healthcare professionals through a seamless digital experience.

As healthcare becomes increasingly data-driven, users often struggle to understand medical reports, symptoms, prescriptions, and diagnostic images without professional guidance. AI Doctor bridges this gap by combining **Artificial Intelligence**, **OCR-based medical report scanning**, **NLP-powered symptom analysis**, and intelligent doctor discovery into one privacy-focused platform.

By analyzing medical documents, extracting important health information, understanding user-provided symptoms, and offering AI-powered insights, the platform helps users make more informed decisions before consulting a healthcare professional. Users can securely upload medical reports, scan X-rays, chat with an AI medical assistant, and find doctors nearby based on location and healthcare needs.

Built with a focus on speed, accuracy, accessibility, and a premium user experience, AI Doctor empowers individuals to better understand their health information and take the next step toward proper medical care.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Medical Report Scanning OCR:** Upload medical documents and extract structured health information using OCR.
- **Symptom Analysis NLP:** Enter symptoms in natural language and receive AI-powered health insights.
- **X-ray Analysis:** Upload X-ray images for AI-assisted visual analysis and preliminary insights.
- **AI Medical Chat Assistant:** Have secure, session-based conversations with a context-aware AI doctor assistant.
- **Doctor Finder:** Discover nearby healthcare professionals using smart recommendations and geo-location.
- **Privacy-Focused Experience:** Designed with secure uploads, protected sessions, and responsible AI usage.
- **Modern UI/UX:** Clean, responsive, and healthcare-themed interface built for trust and simplicity.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Database:** Drizzle ORM
- **AI Integration:** OpenAI
- **OCR:** Medical document scanning and text extraction
- **NLP:** Symptom extraction and AI-based health analysis
- **Image Analysis:** X-ray upload and AI-powered insights
- **Authentication:** Secure user session management
- **Deployment:** Vercel
